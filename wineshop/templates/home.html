About Vino Elite

Founded in 1985, Vino Elite has been at the forefront of wine curation and distribution for over three decades. We specialize in sourcing the finest wines from prestigious vineyards around the world, ensuring that every bottle in our collection meets the highest standards of quality and authenticity.

Our team of certified sommeliers and wine experts travel globally to discover exceptional vintages, from legendary Bordeaux estates to emerging boutique wineries. We believe that great wine brings people together and creates unforgettable moments.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you ensure the authenticity of your wines?

We work directly with vineyards and authorized distributors, maintaining strict chain of custody documentation. Each bottle is verified through our proprietary authentication process, and we provide certificates of authenticity for all premium wines.

What are your shipping and storage conditions?

All wines are stored in temperature-controlled environments at 55°F with 70% humidity. We ship using specialized wine packaging with temperature protection and insurance coverage for all premium bottles.

Do you offer wine tasting events?

Yes! We host monthly wine tasting events featuring different regions and vintages. Our certified sommeliers guide you through the tasting experience, sharing insights about each wine's unique characteristics and history.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unopened bottles in original packaging. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, we'll provide a full refund or exchange. Damaged bottles during shipping are replaced immediately at no cost.

Do you offer wine consultation services?

Absolutely! Our team of certified sommeliers provides personalized wine consultation services, including cellar planning, food pairing recommendations, and custom wine selection for special events and occasions.
